# Orbix-Global

Orbix Global is a business networking and collaboration platform designed to bring together the best features of LinkedIn, business marketplaces, and modern collaboration software into one ecosystem.



Our goal is to create a single platform where entrepreneurs, business owners, investors, professionals, and companies can connect, collaborate, and grow.



Users will be able to:



* List businesses for sale.

* Find investors or investment opportunities.

* Sell equity or ownership stakes in their businesses.

* Connect with strategic partners.

* Recruit talent and discover new business opportunities.

* Build valuable relationships with professionals worldwide.



A key feature of Orbix Global is the Digital Office—a fully integrated collaborative workspace where teams can work together from anywhere. Each Digital Office includes secure video meetings, screen sharing, collaborative whiteboards, shared notes, file storage, task management, and real-time document collaboration, allowing businesses to manage projects without relying on multiple separate platforms.



To encourage trust and reward meaningful contributions, Orbix Global uses a transparent reputation system. Every successful deal, completed project, business introduction, or verified contribution earns users reputation points. For example, each completed transaction or meaningful business collaboration awards 5 points. Over time, these points build a user’s credibility, making it easier for others to identify experienced, reliable, and trusted professionals within the community.



Orbix Global isn’t just another networking platform—it’s an all-in-one business ecosystem where companies can raise capital, buy and sell businesses, collaborate with teams, build trusted relationships, and unlock new opportunities for growth on a global scale.
